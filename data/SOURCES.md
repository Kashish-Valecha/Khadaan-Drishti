# Seed data provenance

`mine_catalog.json` is a curated subset of 24 operating Indian mines from the supplied **Global Coal Mine Tracker, August 2026** workbook in `gem-data.zip`. It retains mine name, owner, state, district, mine type, GEM identifier, and reported production for context only.

The app deliberately does not interpret production or tracker records as compliance evidence. Scores, checklist outcomes, documents, audit entries, and alerts are generated for this demo and are clearly labeled synthetic in the interface.

Checklist orientation sources:

- DGMS, [Coal Mines Regulations, 2017](https://www.dgms.gov.in/writereaddata/UploadFile/CoalMinesRegulation2017.pdf).
- DGMS, [Mines Safety and allied legislation](https://www.dgms.gov.in/UserView?mid=1264).
- PARIVESH/MoEFCC environmental-clearance conditions such as [six-monthly compliance, monitoring, and closure-plan requirements](https://parivesh.nic.in/utildoc/1227018318_1778256652717-signed.pdf).

These sources orient the demo checklist. They are not a substitute for a mine-specific legal review or the current approval conditions.
